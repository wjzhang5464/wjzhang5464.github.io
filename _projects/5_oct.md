Before moving to single-photon imaging, I worked with [Prof. Zhao Wang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=vr4K5noAAAAJ&hl=en) at UESTC on **optical coherence tomography** system development — building and characterizing the optical and signal-processing chain of an OCT setup.

The through-line to my current work is the same one that drew me to computational imaging: the interesting part is rarely the optics alone or the algorithm alone, but the place where a physical measurement model meets an estimation problem.
